_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Bubble Harvest is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

## The short version

- You don't need an account, and the game never asks for your name, email or contacts.
- Your progress stays on your phone.
- The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the play screen, and short videos you can choose to watch for extra pins. Google may use information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
- Where the law asks for it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded. There you can change your choice at any time in Settings → More options → Privacy choices.

## Information kept on your phone

- **Your progress and settings**: levels, stars, Seeds, the garden and your settings are saved in the game's own storage on your phone. They are not sent to us.
- **A play log**: the game keeps a small log of play events (levels started and finished, shots, scores) on your phone to help improve the game. It is never sent anywhere automatically. If you choose **Share playtest log** in Settings, the game opens your phone's share sheet so you can decide where to send it.

Uninstalling the game deletes this information. Settings → More options → Reset progress also erases your progress.

## Ads from Google AdMob

The banner at the bottom of the play screen, and any short video you choose to watch for extra pins, come from Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as:

- your device's advertising ID, device type and operating system,
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- how you interact with the ads.

Depending on your consent and your device settings, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this information is described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

### Consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game uses Google's consent tool to ask for your choice before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → More options → Privacy choices.

## Children

Bubble Harvest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
